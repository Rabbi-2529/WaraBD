Our story

Keys get lost. Codes don't.

WaRa BD started with a simple frustration: the little metal key for a drawer or cabinet is always the one that goes missing. Padlocks get cut, spare keys get copied, and shared lockers turn into a guessing game.

So we focused on one product and set out to do it properly — the WaRa FT-SCL029 keypad lock. It brings code-based, keyless security to the furniture people use every day, at a price that makes sense for homes as well as hotels, offices and gyms.

Today we deliver genuine WaRa locks across Bangladesh, help customers choose the right setup, and stay on call long after the parcel arrives.
